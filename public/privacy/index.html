Privacy

Scan Repair Local is designed to process documents on your device. We do not receive, store, analyse, or sell the pages you open, the recognised text, or the exports you create.

What stays on your device

Document files and OCR results live in the app/browser memory unless you export them. A license token, if you restore one, is stored in local browser/app storage and is checked at most once per day with Sociobot’s licensing service. We do not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the product owner through the project repository.
